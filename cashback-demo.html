Earn cashback on your tab

A demo of how the early-payment cashback plays out for a customer, hour by hour.

This is a test/demo page only — a self-contained simulation, not connected to any real account or Firestore data.

The clock runs a fixed 7am–3pm window every day (8 one-hour segments, 2 real seconds each) and never pauses or resets for anyone - it just keeps going, day after day (cycling every 3 days here).

Your rate depends on how long your own balance has been open: fresh today earns 10%, one day old earns 5%, two or more days old earns nothing - paying it off in full and starting a new balance resets that count, whatever day the clock happens to be on.

The cashback offer only appears while there's an actual balance to pay off.

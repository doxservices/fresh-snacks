This is a test/demo page only — a self-contained simulation, not connected to any real account or Firestore data.

Cashback runs on a fixed 7am–3pm window every day (8 one-hour segments). The same schedule applies to every customer, regardless of when they make a purchase or payment.

A rate reached by 3pm holds steady overnight until 7am the next day — 2 real seconds per segment, so one day's window plays out in 16 seconds.

Past 4 days it loops into a fresh cycle automatically, so testing can continue — nothing reverts to the starting state until you hit Reset.

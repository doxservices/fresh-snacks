A demo of how the early-payment cashback plays out for a customer, hour by hour.

This is a test/demo page only - a self-contained simulation, not connected to any real account or Firestore data. The clock only runs through each day's 8am-3pm window (8 bricks), jumping straight to the next day's 8am once one closes - 2 real seconds per brick, so one day's window plays out in 16 seconds. Past 4 days it loops into a fresh cycle automatically so testing can continue - nothing reverts to the starting state until you hit Reset.
